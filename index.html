🔗 Overlap & Correlation Analysis

How do Expired and Active datasets relate to each other?
⭕ CompGUID Overlap (Venn Diagram)
What this shows: How many companies (CompGUIDs) exist in ONLY expired data (full churn), ONLY active data (healthy), or BOTH datasets (partial churn/overlap).
📊 Customer Flow Breakdown
What this shows: Proportional bar chart showing how your total customer base splits into Healthy, Partial Churn, Full Churn, and At-Risk categories.
🍩 Status Distribution
What this shows: Percentage breakdown of company health. Larger green = healthier customer base.
🔗 Correlation Strength Indicators
What this shows: How strongly different data points correlate. Higher = stronger connection between metrics.

📅 Expiry Timeline & Trend Analysis

When are customers expiring? What's the monthly pattern?
📈 Monthly Expiry vs Active Trend
What this shows: Month-by-month comparison of expired vs active subscriptions. Red bars = expired that month. Green bars = active subscriptions expiring that month. Look for patterns — are expirations increasing over time?
⚠️ At-Risk Customers (Nearing Expiry)
What this shows: Active customers grouped by how many days until their subscription expires. Leftmost bars = most urgent (expiring very soon!). These need immediate renewal calls.
📊 Subscriptions per Company
What this shows: How many subscriptions each company has. Companies with multiple subscriptions = higher revenue per company. If they churn, the loss is bigger.
📉 Cumulative Churn Trend
What this shows: Running total of expired subscriptions over time. A steepening curve = churn is accelerating. A flattening curve = churn is slowing down (good!).

🏢 GSTIN, Company & Geographic Analysis

Which states, entities and segments are most affected?
🔍 GSTIN Data Availability
What this shows: How many records have GSTIN available. Records without GSTIN are harder to track at entity level.
🎯 Churn Risk Radar
What this shows: Multi-factor radar showing different risk dimensions. Larger area = higher overall risk.
🗺️ State-wise Churn Analysis
What this shows: Red bars = expired subscriptions per state. Green bars = active subscriptions per state. Yellow line = churn rate percentage per state. High yellow line = that state needs attention!
🏆 Top 10 Churning States
What this shows: States ranked by churn rate (highest first). Focus retention efforts on these states.
📊 Expired vs Active Scatter
What this shows: Each dot = a state. X-axis = expired count, Y-axis = active count. States above the diagonal line have more active than expired (healthy). States below have more expired (concerning).
